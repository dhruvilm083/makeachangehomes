The Inspiration

All good things start with a spark of ingenious. Make A Change Homes started the same way. We wanted to change the basic standard of the boilerplate, and duplicate construction projects across the industry. People weren't getting value from their investments, and Andrew wanted to Make A Change. That's how the inspiration for Make A Change Homes began, and here we are now, transforming construction, one building at a time.

Core values in every project

Make A Change Homes operates with a few core values in every project we have worked on, and every project we will complete. They are essential to our business and our operations and are fundamental to every single piece of work completed;

  • New ideas; we implement original ideas that align to your specifications that are detailed through every step
  • Modern design; with every new project, we craft innovative, creative designs that are matched to your perfection
  • Advanced material use; in every project we construct, we source state-of-the-art materials available in the market, to ensure the finest quality of our work
  • Best value; we offer competitive pricing and outstanding construction, in every project
